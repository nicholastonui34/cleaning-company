    Ask on WhatsApp
    How long does a sofa take to dry?

    Usually 4 to 6 hours with windows open or a fan running. Our machines extract most of the water, so you can sit on it by evening. Leather is wipe-cleaned and conditioned, and is ready right away.

    Do I need to be home during the clean?

    For first visits we prefer someone is present to show us around. For repeat clients, Airbnb units and move-outs, you can leave keys with the caretaker or a lockbox. We send before and after photos either way.

    What's included in a move-in or move-out clean?

    Inside all cabinets and wardrobes, kitchen degreasing, oven and fridge, bathroom descaling, windows and tracks, skirting boards, light switches, balcony and a full floor scrub. It's the standard a landlord or agent checks against at handover.

    How do I pay?

    M-Pesa (Till number shared on confirmation), bank transfer or cash. Standard jobs are paid after you inspect the work. Move-in/move-out jobs above KES 15,000 take a 30% booking deposit to hold the date.

    Are your products safe for children and pets?

    Yes. We use biodegradable, low-residue detergents and rinse thoroughly. Tell us about allergies or sensitive fabrics when booking and we'll adjust.

    Which areas do you cover?

    Westlands, Kilimani, Kileleshwa and Parklands at no transport charge. We also serve Lavington, Riverside, Spring Valley, Lower Kabete and nearby areas for a flat KES 500 transport fee.
