# HavenCraft Cleaners: Local SEO & Marketing Launch Manual

**Market:** Westlands, Kilimani, Kileleshwa, Parklands (Nairobi)
**Goal for the first 90 days:** 40 paying jobs, 25 Google reviews, 3 Airbnb retainer clients.

---

## Step 1. Google Business Profile (GMB) Setup

Your Google Business Profile drives most "near me" leads. Searches like *sofa cleaning Kilimani* and *move in cleaning Westlands* show the Map Pack above every website, so this comes first.

### 1.1 Create and verify

1. Go to `business.google.com` and sign in with a business Gmail you will keep for years (for example `havencraftcleaners@gmail.com`), not a personal one.
2. **Business name:** `HavenCraft Cleaners`. Use the real registered name only. Adding keywords like "HavenCraft Cleaners Sofa Cleaning Kilimani" gets profiles suspended.
3. **Business type:** choose *Service area business*. Hide your street address if you work from home or a small store.
4. **Service areas:** add Westlands, Kilimani, Kileleshwa, Parklands, Lavington, Riverside and Spring Valley. Keep it under 20 km from your base.
5. **Phone:** your Safaricom business line (same number as WhatsApp). **Website:** your live domain.
6. **Verification:** Google in Kenya now mostly uses **video verification**. Prepare before you start recording:
   - Show the street sign or a landmark near your base, then your premises or vehicle.
   - Show branded equipment: extraction machine, labelled detergent bottles, uniform with logo.
   - Show proof of operation: business permit (Nairobi County Single Business Permit), KRA PIN certificate, or a company stamp.
   - Record in one continuous take, no cuts.
7. Verification usually takes 3 to 7 working days. Do not edit the name, address or category while it is pending.

### 1.2 Categories

| Slot | Category |
|---|---|
| Primary | **Carpet cleaning service** (strongest match for sofa and carpet searches) |
| Secondary | House cleaning service |
| Secondary | Upholstery cleaning service |
| Secondary | Cleaning service |
| Secondary | Janitorial service (only if you later add offices) |

Test the primary category after 60 days: if most calls ask for move-out cleans, switch primary to *House cleaning service* and compare call volume.

### 1.3 Services and products

Add every service with its starting price in KES. Use the same names as the website:
- Sofa Set Deep Extraction Cleaning (from KES 2,500)
- Carpet & Rug Steam Cleaning (from KES 1,500)
- Move-In / Move-Out Deep Cleaning (from KES 8,000)
- Airbnb & B&B Rapid Turnaround (from KES 2,000)
- Routine Home Maintenance (from KES 3,500)

Write a 2 to 3 sentence description for each that naturally names a suburb, for example: *"Hot-water extraction for fabric and suede sofas in Kilimani, Kileleshwa and Westlands. Dry in 4 to 6 hours."*

### 1.4 Photo upload strategy

Google ranks active profiles higher, and photos are the main conversion driver for cleaning businesses.

- **Day 1:** logo (square, 720 × 720), cover photo (team in uniform beside the van or machine), 10 job photos.
- **Every week after:** 3 to 5 new photos. Set a recurring Monday reminder.
- **Photo mix:**
  - Before and after pairs, same angle, same lighting (most important)
  - The dirty extraction water tank (this is what makes people book)
  - Team members in uniform at work, faces visible, with their consent
  - Equipment close-ups
  - Recognisable exterior shots of estates you serve (no unit numbers, no client faces without permission)
- Take photos on the phone with location services on. Name files descriptively before upload, for example `sofa-cleaning-kilimani-before.jpg`.
- Upload short videos too (10 to 30 seconds). The extraction stroke clips from Step 4 work here.

### 1.5 Reviews engine

Reviews are the single biggest local ranking factor you control.

1. In GBP, copy your **"Ask for reviews"** short link.
2. Send it on WhatsApp within 2 hours of every job, while the client is still happy:

> Hi {Name}, thank you for having HavenCraft today. If you're happy with your {sofa/carpet/home}, a quick Google review helps a small Nairobi team a lot: {link}. Mentioning your area and the service helps others find us. Asante!

3. Reply to every review within 24 hours, mention the service and suburb in your reply ("Glad the L-shape in Kileleshwa came out well, Wanjiru").
4. Never buy reviews or offer discounts in exchange for 5 stars. Google removes them and can suspend the profile. Asking every client is enough.

> **Before launch:** the testimonials on the website are layout placeholders. Replace them with real reviews (with the client's consent) as they come in. Publishing invented reviews is misleading under Kenya's Competition Act consumer protection rules.

### 1.6 Weekly posting

Publish one Google Post per week: an offer, a before/after, or a tip ("How to keep a cream sofa clean with kids"). Add a "Book" button linking to WhatsApp.

### 1.7 Citations (NAP consistency)

List the identical **N**ame, **A**rea and **P**hone on: Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Facebook page, Instagram bio, Yellow Pages Kenya, BusinessList.co.ke, Jiji (service listing) and Kenya business directories. Any mismatch in phone format weakens ranking, so pick one format (`0700 000 000`) and use it everywhere.

## Step 2. Hyper-Local WhatsApp & Estate Group Outreach

WhatsApp groups are where Nairobi residents ask "anyone know a good sofa cleaner?" Two rules keep you from being removed:

1. **Read the group rules first.** Many groups only allow adverts on certain days (often "Marketplace Monday" or Fridays). Ask the admin if unsure.
2. **Post once per week maximum per group,** and always reply to direct requests within 10 minutes. Responding fast to someone else's question converts better than any advert.

Always attach 1 or 2 before/after photos. Posts with images get far more replies.

## Step 3. Property Manager & Airbnb Co-Host Partnerships

Retainer clients smooth out cash flow. One host with 4 units averaging 3 turnovers a week is roughly 48 jobs a month.

### Who to target

- Airbnb hosts with 2+ listings in Kilimani, Westlands, Kileleshwa (search Airbnb for those areas, note "Superhost" and multi-listing hosts).
- Co-hosting and short-let management companies (search Instagram and Google for "Airbnb management Nairobi").
- Property agents and caretakers in apartment blocks along Argwings Kodhek, Ngong Road, Rhapta Road, General Mathenge and Riverside Drive. They need move-out cleans every time a tenant leaves.

### Cold outreach script (WhatsApp or Instagram DM)

> Hi {Name}, I saw your {unit name} listing in {Kilimani}. Lovely space, and the reviews show your guests care about cleanliness.
>
> I run HavenCraft Cleaners, a vetted turnaround team covering Kilimani, Westlands and Kileleshwa. We work specifically with hosts:
>
> • We fit your checkout and check-in window (including same-day)
> • Linen change, bathroom sanitisation and amenities restock every time
> • You get a timestamped photo report of every room before the guest arrives
> • One WhatsApp contact, consistent team, invoiced monthly by M-Pesa or bank
>
> **Retainer pricing:**
> • 8 to 15 turnarounds a month: 10% off our standard rate
> • 16+ turnarounds a month: 15% off, plus one free sofa deep clean per unit each quarter
>
> Would you let us do your next turnaround at 20% off as a trial? If the photos and the guest reviews don't impress you, no obligation.
>
> {Name}, HavenCraft Cleaners | 0700 000 000

### Follow-up sequence

| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| 0 | Send the script above |
| 3 | "Hi {Name}, just following up. Here's a before/after from a 2-bed we turned around in Westlands this week." (attach photo report sample) |
| 10 | "Last note from me. If you ever need an emergency turnaround (cancelled cleaner, late checkout), save this number. We keep a slot open most days." |

Stop after 3 touches. Note responses in your sheet.

### Close the deal properly

Put every retainer in a one-page agreement: rate per unit size, response time, damage and breakage policy, linen handling, key handover, payment terms (for example net 7 days), and 14-day notice to cancel. This protects both sides and makes you look established.

## Step 4. Organic Social Content: 5 TikTok / Instagram Reel Concepts

Dirty-to-clean extraction videos are some of the most watched content in the cleaning niche. Film vertical (9:16) on a phone, natural daylight, and keep the first 2 seconds as the most satisfying frame.

**Every video:** caption ends with the suburb plus "Book on WhatsApp, link in bio". Hashtags: `#NairobiCleaning #SofaCleaningNairobi #Kilimani #Westlands #Kileleshwa #NairobiAirbnb #CleanTok #SatisfyingClean #Nairobi`.

### Reel 1: "The Stroke"
- **Hook (0 to 2s):** extreme close-up of the extraction wand pulling one clean stripe across a filthy cream sofa.
- **Body:** 6 to 8 slow overlapping strokes, ASMR sound of the machine (no music for the first 5 seconds).
- **Payoff:** zoom out to the whole sofa finished.
- **On-screen text:** "2 years of Kilimani life on this sofa 😳"
- **CTA:** "3-seater: KES 2,500. Book via WhatsApp."

### Reel 2: "Guess the Tank"
- **Hook:** hold up the clear recovery tank (empty) and ask "How dirty do you think this 5-seater is?"
- **Body:** fast time-lapse of cleaning the full sofa.
- **Payoff:** reveal the brown tank water next to a glass of clean water.
- **On-screen text:** "Comment your guess: 1 to 10 🤢"
- **Why it works:** comments boost reach, and the tank is visual proof your clean is deeper than a DIY wipe.

### Reel 3: "Deposit Back"
- **Hook:** "My landlord said I'd lose my deposit 👀"
- **Body:** split-screen before/after of oven, bathroom tiles, kitchen cabinets and windows in a Westlands move-out.
- **Payoff:** agent doing the walk-through and giving a thumbs up (with permission), or a text overlay "Full deposit returned ✅".
- **CTA:** "Moving this month? 1 to 2 bed from KES 8,000."

### Reel 4: "10am to 2pm Airbnb Turnaround"
- **Hook:** on-screen clock "10:00 Guest checks out" over a messy bedroom.
- **Body:** fast cuts: stripping beds, bathroom scrub, folding towels into shapes, amenities restock, a timer counting up.
- **Payoff:** clock "13:42 Ready", slow pan of the styled unit.
- **On-screen text:** "Hosts in Kilimani, this is what your guests walk into."
- **CTA:** "Retainer rates for hosts. DM us."

### Reel 5: "The Rug Reveal"
- **Hook:** a heavily stained rug, then drag your hand to show the dust.
- **Body:** pre-treat spray, agitation brush, steam pass, time-lapse of drying.
- **Payoff:** ripple effect as you flip the cleaned rug, or a split-screen half-cleaned shot.
- **On-screen text:** "Is your rug actually grey, or just dirty?"
- **CTA:** "Rugs from KES 1,500."

### Posting rhythm

- **TikTok:** 1 video a day for the first 30 days (reuse angles from the same job across multiple videos).
- **Instagram Reels:** 4 a week, same clips, plus Stories with client WhatsApp feedback screenshots (names hidden, with permission).
- **Best times (Nairobi):** 7am to 8am, 12:30pm to 1:30pm, 8pm to 10pm.
- **Weekly review:** keep the hook style of whatever got the most 3-second views, drop the rest.
